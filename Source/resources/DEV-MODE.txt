Developer mode is disabled by default.

After launching once, close the game and open catherine-settings.ini.
Under [Catherine], change devmenu=false to devmenu=true, then restart.
For RecompLauncher or Quiver, edit the INI in the launcher's recompdata folder.
For a standalone installation, edit the INI beside the executable.

Press F8 to open or close the developer picker. You can also start with
"Catherine DEV.cmd" on Windows or ./"Catherine DEV.sh" on Linux to open the
picker at startup. Developer mode must be enabled in the INI first.

Keep dev-checkpoints.pak beside Catherine.exe (Windows) or catherine (Linux).
It supplies the checkpoints required for direct puzzle, bar, landing and room
loading. CUTSCENES.txt and cutscene-catalog.json list the available cutscenes.
You still need assets prepared from your own game and update files.

Set devmenu=false and restart to turn developer mode off again.
